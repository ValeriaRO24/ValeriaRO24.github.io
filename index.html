Research Interests

    Over the course of my academic journey, I've developed a deep interest in the field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), particularly artificial intelligence (AI), and how it can improve and support our lives, especially when it's used thoughtfully and with humanity at its core. I'm particularly drawn to how AI can be used in education, especially for students with learning difficulties or disabilities, and how it can make a meaningful difference in their learning experiences.

    I'm passionate about how emerging technologies are transforming the way we teach, learn, and interact with digital tools. My research delves into the psychological side of educational technology, how students and educators engage with AI and other tools, and how we can improve these interactions to make learning more accessible and effective for all.

    A big part of my research methods involves using databases and analysis to better understand the impact of these technologies. I believe in the power of data-driven insights to shape and improve educational experiences. Alongside this, I also believe in the value of strong software development practices and the thoughtful use of educational platforms and tools. It's exciting to see how, when used responsibly, technology like AI can not only enhance learning but also support student well-being and contribute to creating a more inclusive and supportive educational environment.
